Hi, I'm Duyen 👋

I grow Truecaller, the caller ID and spam-blocking app used by over 500 million people. I own onboarding across Android and iOS: every screen between install and a working caller ID, for new users arriving from 25+ markets every day.

I didn't start in product. I joined the CEO Office in 2022 running company strategy and board reporting, moved into growth analytics, and earned product ownership by shipping. Since then I've run 60+ experiments, built the spam-protection step in onboarding that's now live worldwide, shipped compliance features under legal deadlines in 3 jurisdictions, and moved millions of users off tampered app versions.

What I'm known for: experiments that get killed fast when they don't work, tickets engineers can pick up without a meeting, and being the PM Legal actually likes working with.

Where I'm heading

I want to keep growing at the intersection of growth and trust: owning bigger slices of the user journey, and turning one-off experiments into systems other teams build on. I'm most drawn to products where onboarding, monetization, and privacy all have to work at once, and to using AI to shorten the path from idea to shipped.
