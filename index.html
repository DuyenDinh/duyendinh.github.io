Monetization Onboarding User Research

Block Settings Onboarding Screen

Turning a support complaint into a monetization win

+72% Premium Purchase

Situation

Users were leaving negative reviews saying Truecaller wasn't blocking unwanted spam calls, even though the feature existed. Data showed the root cause: block/protection settings were off by default, and in usability testing, 9 out of 9 users could not find how to turn auto-block on.

Task

Improve the block-activation experience to increase both premium purchase and user satisfaction.

Actions

  • Read through user reviews and support tickets to understand the exact language users used to describe the frustration.
  • Ran UX research (9 test users) confirming the setting was undiscoverable.
  • Designed a single, dedicated onboarding screen that clearly showed the value of blocking and walked users through turning it on, keeping the design deliberately simple and benefit-focused.
  • Iterated the copy and layout based on UX research findings before wide rollout.

Results

  • +72% increase in premium purchase, the new screen replaced the existing premium screen worldwide.
  • The concept was replicated to rebuild the broader in-app block experience.
  • Directly nominated by manager as a standout achievement for user-first thinking.
Monetization Onboarding Experimentation

Premium Choice Onboarding Screen

Finding a monetization win inside someone else's feature territory

+30% Premium Purchase · 0% registration impact

Situation

The growth team needed to increase premium subscriptions, but most premium-related features were owned by a dedicated Premium team, leaving limited room to act quickly within the growth team's own scope.

Task

Find a monetization win inside the onboarding/first-week experience the growth team already owned, without hurting the metric growth is measured on: registration rate.

Actions

  • Investigated where premium value could be surfaced earlier, inside the existing onboarding flow.
  • Added a single new screen explaining the difference between paid and free tiers in plain language, addressing directly why users see ads.
  • Explicitly tested and confirmed the new screen would not reduce registration completion before scaling.

Results

  • +30% increase in premium purchase.
  • Zero impact on registration rate, proving a single well-placed screen can drive monetization without hurting top-of-funnel conversion.
Funnel Optimization UX Global

Onboarding Funnel Optimization

Fixing the "leaking bucket"

+20% Registration Rate (worldwide)

Situation

The onboarding flow behaved like a leaking bucket. Even small drop-offs at the very start of the funnel compounded into significant user loss by the time registration completed, and drop-off rates varied hugely by market.

Task

Establish a continuous-improvement cycle for worldwide registration rate.

Actions

  • Partnered with data analysts to run funnel analysis, pinpointing the two highest drop-off screens.
  • Simplified the UI on those screens to cut cognitive load, and added a confirmation prompt to catch accidental exits.
  • Audited the permission-request screen and moved non-essential permission prompts to after core registration instead of before.
  • Built a fast-track path for returning users to skip redundant steps entirely.
  • Deferred 4 non-mandatory onboarding steps to post-registration days.
  • Localized registration steps and verification methods specifically for focus growth markets.

Results

  • +20% improvement in registration rate worldwide.
  • Funnel moved into a stable maintenance mode after the optimization cycle closed.
Retention Lifecycle CRM Creative

Day 1 Push Notification

Mirroring the user's own words back to them

10% CTR · highest ever recorded

Situation

Users in growth markets (e.g. Colombia, Nigeria) were leaving negative reviews saying Truecaller wasn't blocking unwanted calls, the same root cause as the block-settings case: blocking settings off by default, undiscovered by new users. This one needed a lifecycle/comms fix, not just a UI fix.

Task

Educate new users about blocking settings, at the right moment, in a way that would actually get opened.

Actions

  • Instead of writing generic marketing copy, pulled the exact phrases users had used in their negative reviews and used that language directly in the push notification copy.

Results

  • 10% click-through rate, a record high for any push campaign at Truecaller.
  • Measurable lift in D1/D7 retention.
  • Significant reduction in negative reviews mentioning blocking.
  • Insight directly informed the design of the block-settings onboarding screen.

7-Day New User Retention Experience

Bridging the activation gap

+15pp Day-7 Retention (cumulative, 3 yrs)

Situation

Even among users who completed registration, there was a significant activation gap. Many registered and then went inactive. Industry benchmarks and internal data both pointed to Day-7 retention as the leading indicator of long-term stickiness.

Task

Results

  • Established a lasting framework where the user journey continues well past registration, instead of ending at signup.
Leadership Cross-functional Compliance Trade-off Analysis

App Upgrade Project (≥14.26)

Getting 41M users to move, with 8+ teams aligned

92% Upgrade Rate (target 90%) · 41M+ users

Situation

For legal and messaging-compliance reasons, Truecaller needed 90%+ of its active user base on app version 14.26 or above. At the start of the project, ~13.4% of users (41M+ people) were still below that version, and standard push nudges were converting at only 0.1%.

Task

Lead the project to close the gap and hit the 90% target.

Actions

  • Collaborated with the Release team on a phased Google Play rollout strategy.
  • Partnered with CRM and Brand teams on upgrade push notifications and MassIM campaigns.
  • Tracked upgrade rate and uninstall rate weekly across the full rollout.
  • Investigated a stuck cohort of 1.8M users on a "preload" build who weren't responding to nudges at all. Traced the root cause to nudge logic checking the Play Store version instead of the actual device build version, found it was concentrated on Vivo devices, and worked with the Release team to enable a legacy prompt specifically for that cohort, recovering ~40% of that stuck group.
  • Faced with the 0.1% soft-nudge conversion rate, ran a trade-off analysis estimating a hard (mandatory) upgrade would convert ~40% of remaining users per version at a cost of ~5% net uninstall, and presented this explicitly to stakeholders as a business trade-off (short-term user loss vs. long-term compliance and revenue) to get sign-off.

Results

  • Closed the project at 92% upgrade rate, beating the 90% target, across 41M+ users.
  • Documented lessons learned into a reusable framework for future upgrade projects.
  • Ran this largely independently while direct manager was on leave for 6 months; manager noted on return that key hypotheses had "already been validated with data" before being raised.
Data Investigation Analytics Problem Solving

Root-Cause Investigation: The Frozen App Version

Why were 40,000 users a day still downloading a version from a year ago?

Root cause Systemic fix, not a one-off patch

Situation

Noticed a persistent, non-decaying pattern: roughly 40,000 users per day were newly installing app version 13.34, over a year old, well after multiple newer versions had shipped. This blocked a newer verification method (WhatsApp/ROTP) for those users and was quietly driving up cost and crash rates.

Task

Find out why users kept landing on a version that old, since a normal "old version tail" should decay over time, not stay flat.

Actions

  • Confirmed the pattern was concentrated in specific countries and devices, not randomly distributed.
  • Noted the flat (non-decaying) shape of the trend, which ruled out an ordinary long-tail explanation.
  • Traced install sources and found 85% came via APK, not the Play Store.
  • Looped in the web team to check APK distribution and the release team to check if anything was unusual about the 13.34 build specifically.
  • Root cause: device partners (Transsion/Infinix) in Nigeria had bundled 13.34, the version from a prior brand-refresh campaign, into their devices and simply never updated the bundled APK.

Results

  • Routed the fix to the User Acquisition team to work directly with the device partner on updating their bundled build.
  • Established a longer-term monitoring framework to catch this class of issue earlier next time, rather than relying on someone noticing it by chance.
